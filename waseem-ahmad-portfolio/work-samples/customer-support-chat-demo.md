# Customer support chat — demonstration

**Scenario:** A customer says an order has not arrived.

**Customer:** My order hasn't arrived yet. Can you check?

**Agent:** I'm sorry your order hasn't arrived yet. I can help check this for you. Please share your order number, and I'll review the latest status and let you know the next step.

**Approach shown:** Acknowledge the concern, request only the information needed, explain the next action, and avoid promising an outcome before checking.
